Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

JobLink is a student capstone project built for educational purposes. This page describes how the application actually handles data today, so it doubles as an accurate technical summary as well as a placeholder policy — it has not been reviewed by a lawyer for use in a live commercial product.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Account information — full name, email address, password, and account role (jobseeker or employer).
  • Company information — for employer accounts, your company name.
  • Profile & resume data — work experience, education, skills, certifications, and other resume content you enter or upload.
  • Application data — jobs you apply to, application status, and related notes.
  • Uploaded files — resumes you upload for parsing or ATS analysis.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and secure your account, and authenticate you when you log in.
  • Let you build, edit, and export resumes.
  • Match jobseekers with relevant job listings and let employers review applicants.
  • Generate AI-assisted resume suggestions, ATS scores, and job-match notes.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Service.

3. AI Processing

When you use an AI-assisted feature (for example, "Generate with AI" on the Resume Builder, or ATS/job-match suggestions), the relevant resume or job text you provide is sent to a third-party AI provider (Anthropic's Claude API) to generate a response. Our AI features are instructed to work only from the information you actually supply — they are designed not to invent employers, dates, degrees, or skills on your behalf. AI providers process this data under their own privacy terms; we do not use your resume content to train our own models.

4. How Information Is Shared

  • Employers and jobseekers: when you apply to a job, the employer who posted it can see the application details and relevant resume/profile information you submitted with it.
  • Job search data: job listings displayed in the dashboard may be sourced from a third-party job search API (JSearch/RapidAPI); no personal account information is sent to that provider.
  • AI provider: as described in Section 3, resume/job text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API only when you actively use an AI feature.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Local Storage & Sessions

After you log in, JobLink stores your basic account details and session token in your browser's local storage so you stay logged in between page visits. This data stays on your device and is cleared when you log out or clear your browser storage.

6. Security

Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed using bcrypt before being saved, so even we cannot see your actual password. Traffic between your browser and the JobLink API is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). API keys used by JobLink's AI and job-search integrations are kept on the server side and are never exposed to the browser.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account and profile data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, associated profile, resume, and application data is removed or anonymized within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Your Rights

You can review and update most of your information directly from your Profile and Resume Builder pages at any time. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us as described in Section 11.

9. Children's Privacy

JobLink is intended for users who are at least 16 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to the JobLink team through the contact details listed on the landing page.
